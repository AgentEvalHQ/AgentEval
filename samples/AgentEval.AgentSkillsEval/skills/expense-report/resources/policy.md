# Corporate Meal Expense Policy

**Effective for all domestic and international business travel.**

## Per-meal caps (USD)

| Meal      | Cap    |
|-----------|--------|
| Breakfast | $35    |
| Lunch     | $45    |
| Dinner    | $150   |

## Rules

- Amounts above the cap for a given meal are **not reimbursable** without prior written
  approval from the employee's manager.
- Alcohol is excluded from the meal cap and must be itemized separately; it is reimbursable
  only when entertaining a client, up to $50 per event.
- A receipt is required for any single expense over $25.
- Group meals: the cap applies **per attendee**, not per receipt.

## Overage handling

If a submitted expense exceeds the applicable cap, the overage amount (submitted amount minus
the cap) must be flagged in the expense report and either (a) covered personally by the
employee, or (b) pre-approved by a manager before submission.
